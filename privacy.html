Privacy policy

Story Engine Uploader, for the 3AM Call Stories YouTube channel. Last updated October 31, 2026.

What the app does

Story Engine Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the 3AM Call Stories channel. It signs in to that one YouTube account with Google OAuth and uploads the channel's own videos, thumbnails and captions, and sets their titles, descriptions and publish times.

Data it uses

It requests only the YouTube permissions needed to upload and manage the channel's own videos. The OAuth token is stored on the owner's own computers and is never shared. The app does not collect, sell or share any personal data, and it does not access any other person's account or data.

Google user data

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
